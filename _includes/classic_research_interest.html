Research Interest

My long-term goal is to build general-purpose intelligent systems that learn from experience, reason through open-ended problems, and act reliably in the real world. I believe such systems can work alongside people as partners in discovery and expand the range of problems humanity can solve. Toward this goal, my work centers on three areas:

  • Agentic AI: agents that plan, reason, and act over long horizons.
  • Post-Training: improving language and vision-language models after pretraining through reinforcement learning and self-play.
  • Foundation Models: models that learn at scale from distributed, heterogeneous data.

I extend these methods to applications in weather and climate, medical imaging, remote sensing, and scientific design.
