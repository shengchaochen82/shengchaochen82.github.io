Research Interest

My long-term goal is to build AI systems that learn, reason, and act reliably in the real world, where data is scarce, heterogeneous, and private, and where tasks unfold over long horizons. I study how intelligent agents can gather knowledge across devices, institutions, and modalities and turn it into sound decisions over many steps. I also use these methods to advance science and medicine, with applications in weather and climate, medical imaging, remote sensing, and photonics. My work centers on the following areas:

  • Machine Learning
  • Multimodal Learning
  • Agentic AI
  • Long-Horizon Understanding and Reasoning
  • AI for Scientific Design and Problem Solving
